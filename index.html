Hermes Personal Assistant

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private tool that uses read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar to summarize requested information and prepare reply drafts for human review. It does not send email or change messages or calendar events. Relevant content may be processed by OpenAI for the tasks you request. This is not a public sign-up service. See the privacy policy and terms of use for details.
